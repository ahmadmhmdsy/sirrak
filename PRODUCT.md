## Product Purpose

Sirrak (سِرِّك, "your secret") is a private, offline, Arabic-first AI assistant for Android. It chats, reads the user's documents with citations, and speaks Arabic — entirely on the device: no account, no cloud, no subscription, no telemetry. Success means a mid-range phone gets a working Arabic model, answers about the user's own PDFs, and dictation, without a byte leaving the phone.

## Positioning

The mechanism a neighboring product could not truthfully copy: **a local-first assistant whose first language is Arabic** — RTL-native interface, Arabic typography and voice, dialect-tolerant prompts and eval, document RAG that cites the user's own files, and an auditable zero-network promise — packaged as a free, Apache-2.0, F-Droid-eligible app. PocketPal has no document Q&A and no F-Droid build; cloud assistants cannot make the privacy claim; Arabic-first quality is the moat, not the model.

## Operating Context

- Phone-first, one-handed, often on a bus or in a lecture hall with no signal; airplane mode is the normal state, not a demo.
- Mid-range hardware, 6–8 GB RAM, thermal throttling, 2–4 GB model downloads on metered data (Wi-Fi only by default).
- Mixed Arabic/English life: MSA for writing, Iraqi/Gulf dialect for speech; documents are Arabic PDFs, scanned lecture notes, contracts.
- No account, no onboarding email, no sync. Local chats and documents; export/delete must be obvious.
- The assistant is expected to be honest about its limits (speed, memory, model fit) rather than magical.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Sirrak (سِرِّك)** — Arabic wordmark must render correctly; the Latin wordmark is secondary.
- Voice: calm, precise, honest, quietly confident; Arabic-first copy in Modern Standard Arabic with Iraqi warmth in examples; no hype, no "AI magic", no emoji-speak.
- Privacy as a visible promise: no telemetry, no account, network activity is inspectable.
- Open source (Apache-2.0), F-Droid-friendly, no proprietary blobs in the libre flavor.
- The visual identity — **Girih & Ink** — is established and implemented in the M1 app (ivory/ink/lapis/brass, Amiri + IBM Plex Sans Arabic, one girih mark); the founder delegated design quality to the `impeccable` process. **Theme packs are data-only by contract (shipped in M4): they change grounds, surfaces, accents, error/outline families and the four decorative tones only** — Girih & Ink remains the pinned default, and geometry, Arabic typography, spacing, motion and the lapis-as-action rule are fixed across every pack. A pack that fails the executable contrast gate (20 painted pairs per pack/mode, 4.5:1 body / 3:1 UI) does not ship; Paper ships as the maximum-clarity pack.

## Product Principles

1. **Offline is the default, not a mode.** Every core flow works in airplane mode.
2. **Arabic is a first language, not a locale.** RTL, Arabic typography and voice are the reference implementation; English is the translation.
3. **Honest about capability.** Measured speed, device labels, model limits; explain and degrade, never pretend.
4. **The user owns the data.** Chats and documents stay on the device; one-tap export/delete; no analytics.
5. **Fail soft, fail loud to the developer.** User-facing failure explains and degrades; composition/configuration failures crash debug builds rather than silently losing a feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- TalkBack semantics on every control; 48 dp minimum touch targets; dynamic type support; light/dark modes plus the shipped high-contrast **Paper** theme pack (AAA body text) and in-app font scale (M6); no reliance on colour alone for state.
- Full RTL mirroring with Arabic shaping verified on device; Arabic-Indic digits optional, Western digits for technical data by default.
- Works one-handed on a 6.1–6.5" phone in bright sunlight; no reliance on colour alone; no motion-only communication.
